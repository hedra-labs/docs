In a **simulation study**, Hedra fitted a robot running a trained policy with a
new gripper and restored task success from **near zero to near 100%**, without
retraining the policy.

## How did the adaptation work?

Hedra matched the new gripper's reach and grasp point in the wrist camera view.
This let the trained policy recover task success after the hardware change.

| Condition | Reported outcome |
| --- | --- |
| New gripper before adaptation | Task success near zero |
| New gripper after matching reach and grasp point in the wrist camera view | Task success near 100% |
| Policy retraining | None |
| Evaluation environment | Simulation |

## What does this demonstrate?

The study addresses a specific problem: keeping a trained policy useful when its
robot hardware changes. The result is from simulation, rather than a physical
robot deployment.

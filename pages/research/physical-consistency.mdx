Hedra retrained H3 for physical consistency. The retrained model scored **34.9**
on Google DeepMind's **Physics-IQ** benchmark, compared with **30.3** for H3 Max,
with comparable visual quality.

## What was measured?

| Model | Physics-IQ score |
| --- | --- |
| H3 Max | 30.3 |
| H3 retrained for physical consistency | 34.9 |

The benchmark measures physical consistency in generated video. The reported
visual quality is a separate assessment of the output.

## What was the speed result?

An autonomously distilled version rendered a **five-second clip in under three
seconds on AMD MI355X GPUs**. This is a separate result from the Physics-IQ
comparison above: the 34.9 score is reported for the retrained model.

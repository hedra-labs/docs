---
title: "Edit Video"
description: "Assemble generated and uploaded media into a timed video composition."
---

Edit Video is Hedra's timeline editor, also referred to as Composer. Use it after
inference when the final result needs multiple clips, timing control, audio, or a
single exported file.

## Start or reopen a composition

Open **Manual tools → Edit Video**. Select **New composition** or choose an existing
composition from the list.

## Editing workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add media">
    Bring generated or uploaded video, image, and audio assets into the composition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the sequence">
    Arrange clips in order and trim them to the intended duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage audio">
    Add voice, music, or sound; adjust the relationship between source audio and the
    composition's main track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Watch the complete sequence for pacing, continuity, framing, and audio sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Render the finished composition and review the exported file before sharing it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Agent to Composer

Use a Space to develop the concept and generate the components. Move to Composer
when the creative decisions become editorial: order, duration, rhythm, audio, and
final delivery.

<Tip>
  Name compositions by campaign or deliverable instead of leaving several projects
  with generic titles.
</Tip>

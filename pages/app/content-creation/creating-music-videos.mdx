---

A music video is usually a multi-step project: creative direction, performance
footage, supporting visuals, shot continuity, and editing. Keep those decisions in
one Space so the agent can build on the same song and visual language.

## Choose a structure

- **Performance-led** — the artist or character carries most of the video.
- **Narrative-led** — generated scenes tell a story around the song.
- **Hybrid** — performance anchors the edit while narrative or abstract shots add
  rhythm and scale.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the song">
    Upload the final audio and describe the artist, audience, platform, and intended
    visual direction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan before generating">
    Ask the agent for sections, visual motifs, a beat-aware shot list, or a
    storyboard. Decide where performance and supporting visuals should appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create visual anchors">
    Build Elements for recurring artists, outfits, environments, or styles when
    consistency matters across shots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate short clips">
    Create focused shots with a clear subject action and camera direction. Review
    identity and continuity before producing the next group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit in Composer">
    Assemble the clips, align cuts to the song, adjust timing, and export the final
    composition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Direct a shot

```text
Using @artist and @warehouse, create a 6-second waist-up performance shot.
Slow handheld push-in, green fluorescent practicals, 35mm grain.
The artist performs the second line of the chorus directly to camera.
```

<Tip>
  Lock the visual rules and two or three representative frames before generating
  the full shot list.
</Tip>

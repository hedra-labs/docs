---
title: "Swap a face"
description: "Swap a face between two images with Hedra's swap-a-face Skill using a clear source identity and target image, while following consent and safety rules."
---

Use the **swap-a-face** Skill when you want to transfer a face from one image into
another while preserving the target composition.

<Warning>
  Only use faces you have permission to use. Do not create deceptive,
  non-consensual, or impersonating content.
</Warning>

## Inputs

You need:

- a clear **source face** whose identity should be transferred;
- a **target image** containing the face to replace.

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Skill">
    Open **Personalize → Skills** and choose **swap-a-face**, or select it from the
    available Skill shortcuts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add both references">
    Label the source and target clearly in your instruction so their roles cannot
    be confused.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what to preserve">
    Specify whether expression, hair, pose, lighting, clothing, and background
    should stay from the target image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the result">
    Check identity, edges, skin tone, lighting, and occlusion before using or sharing
    the image.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```text
Use reference 1 as the source identity and reference 2 as the target.
Replace only the face. Preserve the target's hair, expression, lighting,
clothing, pose, and background.
```

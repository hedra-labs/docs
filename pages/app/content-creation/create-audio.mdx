Open **Manual tools → Speech** to generate spoken audio from a script.

<Frame caption="Choose a voice, control the performance, and write the script in one panel.">
  ![Hedra manual Speech tool](/images/product/manual-speech.webp)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a voice">
    Open the voice selector and preview the available voices. Voice availability can
    depend on your workspace and plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the script">
    Enter up to the limit shown in the editor. Use punctuation and paragraph breaks
    to make the intended pacing clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct the delivery">
    Open **Synthesis settings** for voice-specific controls. Select **Control** or
    type `/` to insert supported emotion, reaction, energy, or pacing tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and review">
    Select **Generate**, listen to the result, and adjust the text or synthesis
    controls before using the audio in a Space, avatar video, or composition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Write for speech

- Prefer short sentences and pronounceable wording.
- Spell out uncommon abbreviations when pronunciation matters.
- Use line breaks to separate changes in thought or tone.
- Generate a short sample before committing to a long script.

<Tip>
  When speech is one part of a larger creative task, generate or upload it first,
  then add it as an audio reference in the same Space.
</Tip>

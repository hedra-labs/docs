---

The **ugc** Skill gives the agent a repeatable starting workflow for creator-style
product videos.

## Brief the outcome

Start with the product, audience, platform, promise, and format:

```text
Create a 15-second 9:16 UGC concept for this running shoe.
Audience: recreational runners returning after an injury.
Lead with comfort, keep the product design exact, and end on a clean product shot.
The performance should feel candid, not like a polished television ad.
```

## Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the UGC Skill">
    Choose **ugc** on the home screen or invoke it in a new Space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the product">
    Attach clear product references. Include multiple angles when shape, packaging,
    or logos must remain consistent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Develop the concept">
    Ask the agent for a hook, beats, script, shot plan, or storyboard before running
    expensive video inference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the pieces">
    Approve the direction, then create the presenter, product shots, voice, and
    supporting visuals in the same Space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assemble and review">
    Use Composer when the concept needs multiple clips, timing changes, voiceover,
    or a final export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review checklist

- The first seconds communicate a clear hook.
- The product remains recognizable and accurate.
- The voice and performance fit the intended creator.
- Each shot advances the claim or demonstration.
- The framing and duration match the target platform.
- Claims are supportable and appropriate for the product.

<Tip>
  Generate a storyboard or a small set of still frames before video. It is faster
  to fix the concept before motion is involved.
</Tip>

---
title: "Drive video with motion references"
description: "Use a reference video to transfer useful motion while preserving the intended character or scene."
---

Some video models accept a motion or video reference. Use that workflow when the
movement matters more than a text description alone can communicate.

## Inputs

- a clear visual reference for the character or scene;
- a motion reference with the action and camera behavior you want;
- a prompt explaining what should transfer and what should remain unchanged.

## Manual workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Video">
    Go to **Manual tools → Video** and choose a model that accepts a video or motion
    reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add references">
    Add the character image or start frame and the reference video required by the
    selected model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the transfer">
    Name the subject, motion, framing, environment, and the details that must remain
    faithful to the visual reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose supported settings">
    Set the available aspect ratio, resolution, duration, and batch size.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and compare">
    Review body geometry, identity, contact points, and camera motion. Try a simpler
    reference if the movement is unclear or partially occluded.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Motion-reference support and controls are model-specific. Use the live selector
  rather than relying on a fixed model name.
</Note>

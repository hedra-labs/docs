---
title: "Animate a still image"
description: "Bring a still image to life with agent direction or direct video inference."
---

Animation works best when the prompt describes motion and camera behavior without
contradicting the source image.

## Animate with the agent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start with the animate Skill">
    Select **animate** on the home screen or start a new Space in **Video** mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the still image">
    Attach the image as a reference and explain which parts must remain unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Direct motion">
    Describe subject motion, environmental motion, camera movement, pace, and the
    intended duration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review in context">
    Check identity, geometry, and temporal consistency. Ask for one bounded change
    at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

```text
Animate this portrait with subtle breathing and one natural blink.
The camera makes a slow 5% push-in. Keep the face, wardrobe, background,
and lighting unchanged. No speaking.
```

## Animate with the manual Video tool

Open **Manual tools → Video**, choose a model that accepts an image reference,
attach the still, and set the supported duration, resolution, and aspect ratio.

<Tip>
  Small, physically plausible motion is easier to preserve than several competing
  actions in one short clip.
</Tip>

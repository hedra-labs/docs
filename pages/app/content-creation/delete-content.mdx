---
title: "Manage and delete content"
description: "Understand how Hedra stores work across Spaces, Library assets, Projects, and Elements, and delete each one safely without breaking active workflows."
---

Hedra stores work in several related places:

- a **Space** contains an agent conversation, canvas, and its working context;
- a **Library asset** is an image, video, or audio file;
- a **Project** is an organizational collection;
- an **Element** is reusable visual context.

Deleting one kind of object is not the same as removing another.

## Delete a Space

On the home page, open the Space card's action menu and choose **Delete**. Hedra
shows a final warning before permanently deleting the Space and all its contents.

## Delete an asset

Open **Library → My work**, open the asset or its action menu, and choose the delete
action. Confirm only after checking that no active workflow still depends on it.

## Delete a Project or composition

Use the action menu on the Project or Composer card. Removing an organizational
container may have different effects from deleting the underlying media, so read
the confirmation dialog carefully.

<Warning>
  Deletion is permanent. Download anything you may need later and verify the exact
  target before confirming.
</Warning>

## Prefer organization when the work is still useful

Use Projects, favorites, search, and clear names when the problem is clutter rather
than unwanted data.

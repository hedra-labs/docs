Hedra offers an Ambassador Program for content creators, influencers, and partners who are interested in collaborating with our team to shape the future of the platform.

## How to Apply

If you're interested in joining the Ambassador Program, apply at [partners.dub.co/hedra](https://partners.dub.co/hedra).

<Note>
  Due to the high volume of submissions, it may take 1–2 weeks to review your application.
</Note>

## Ambassador Program Benefits

If your application is approved, you'll receive:

- Early access to new tools and features
- Commission from affiliate links
- Free plans and credits
- Features on Hedra's main social media accounts
- Opportunities to collaborate directly with the Hedra team

## Enterprise Partnerships

For larger-scale partnerships or enterprise integrations, please reach out to the sales team at [sales@hedra.com](mailto:sales@hedra.com) with details about your specific requirements and how you envision Hedra fitting into your platform or business needs.

---
title: "Delete Your Account"
description: "Delete your Hedra account from profile settings and understand why deletion is permanent, with no way to recover the account or its content afterwards."
---

## Deleting your account

Navigate to [www.hedra.com/app/settings/profile](https://www.hedra.com/app/settings/profile), select **Delete your account**, and confirm.

## Recovering a deleted account

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent. If you delete your account, you will need to create a new account to access Hedra.
</Warning>

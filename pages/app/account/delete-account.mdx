## Deleting your account

Navigate to [www.hedra.com/app/settings/privacy](https://www.hedra.com/app/settings/privacy), select **Delete account**, and confirm.

The [Hedra Privacy Policy](https://www.hedra.com/privacy) describes what happens to your data when you delete your account.

How an annual subscription delivers credits depends on the plan.

## Basic, Pro, and Ultra

Annual Basic, Pro, and Ultra plans are billed once a year and grant credits one month at a time. You receive the first month's credits when you pay, and the next month's credits on the same day each following month.

For example, an annual plan that includes 7,200 credits per month grants 7,200 credits when you subscribe and another 7,200 on each monthly anniversary, for 12 grants in the year.

Each month works like a monthly plan: subscription credits you don't use expire when the next month's credits arrive. Credits from credit packs, and your API wallet balance, carry over.

## Older annual plans

Annual Creator, Professional, and earlier Basic plans grant all 12 months of credits upfront when you pay. A plan with 4,000 credits per month receives 48,000 credits at purchase, to use at any pace during the year.

## Getting Additional Credits

If you run low on credits before your next grant or renewal, you can:

- **Upgrade** to a higher-tier plan at [www.hedra.com/pricing](https://www.hedra.com/pricing)
- **Purchase** a one-time credit pack

An annual Basic, Pro or Ultra subscription bills the full year at once and grants your credits one month at a time.

## Credit Distribution

You receive your plan's monthly credits when you pay, then again on the same day each month for the rest of the year.

For example, an annual plan that includes 7,200 credits per month grants 7,200 credits at purchase and 7,200 more on each monthly anniversary, 12 grants in all.

Unused plan credits expire when the next month's credits arrive, as they do on a monthly plan. Credits from credit packs carry over.

<Note>
  Annual plans bought before the current Basic, Pro and Ultra plans (for example Creator and Professional) still grant the full year of credits upfront.
</Note>

## Getting Additional Credits

If you run low on credits before your next monthly grant, you have two options:

- **Upgrade** to a higher-tier plan at [www.hedra.com/pricing](https://www.hedra.com/pricing)
- **Purchase** a one-time credit pack

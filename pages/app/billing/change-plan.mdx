title: "Change Your Plan or Buy Credits"
description: "Change your Hedra subscription plan or buy one-time credit packs from the Billing page, and confirm pricing and effective dates before purchase."
---

Open [Billing](https://www.hedra.com/app/subscription) while signed in to manage your subscription and credits.

## Change your plan

1. Review the plans and billing intervals available to your account.
2. Choose a plan.
3. Review the price and effective date before confirming.

The confirmation screen is the source of truth for when a change takes effect. If you need help with an existing subscription, contact Support before confirming another purchase.

## Buy additional credits

If your account is eligible for credit packs, choose **Buy credits**, select a pack, and review the checkout total before confirming. Purchased credits normally appear as soon as payment completes.

<Note>
  Available plans, billing intervals, credit packs, and prices can vary. Use the current Billing page rather than screenshots or older price tables.
</Note>

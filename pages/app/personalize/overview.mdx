---

**Personalize** is where you tailor your Hedra agent to your workflow. It's split into two tabs:

- **Skills** — reusable instructions your agent can invoke on demand (yours plus a catalog from Hedra).
- **Connectors** — third-party services (via MCP) that your agent can read from and act on during a session.

## Open Personalize

Click **Personalize** in the left sidebar (mobile: open the menu and tap **Personalize**). The page opens on the **Skills** tab by default; switch to **Connectors** using the tabs at the top.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card icon="sparkles" href="/pages/app/personalize/skills" title="Skills">
    Create private skills, browse the Hedra catalog, and edit or delete your own.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="plug" href="/pages/app/personalize/connectors" title="Connectors">
